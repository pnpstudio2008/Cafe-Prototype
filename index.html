OUR STORY

WE BREW CHAOS INTO ART.

Founded in 2021, KINETIC & CO. was built for people who refuse boring routine coffee. We source single-origin specialty beans directly from micro-farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting every batch right in front of your eyes.

Whether you need a lightning-fast double espresso before work or a slow drip cold brew surrounded by vibrant design and good tunes, we are your daily sanctuary.
